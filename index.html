What the trajectory panel is
actually compressing

The "thunderclap → Alps → Greek serenity" sequence above isn't invented shorthand — it's a compression of Nietzsche's own account, written as the new 1886 preface to Birth of Tragedy. He dates the book's real origin to the Franco-Prussian War: he was drafting his thoughts on the Greeks in an Alpine retreat in the summer of 1870 as news of the fighting reached him, was still turning the same question over weeks later near Metz, and only resolved it — convalescing from an illness picked up in the field — in the same month Prussia and France were negotiating terms at Versailles. The peace table and the manuscript table close in the same month, in the same breath.

"the thunder of the battle of Wörth was rolling over Europe" Nietzsche, Attempt at a Self-Criticism (1886), §1
